How scam protection works

  • One switch covers every scam protection listed on the right (hover a line for what it does).
  • Each protection blocks or warns on its own the moment it sees the scam (from level ).
  • Redirects and dialogs from frames are blocked from level , outside safer regions.
  • “Always allow” or “Always ignore” in a scam warning adds “Scam: site” to your allow list.

How tamper protection works

  • Tamper detection is started by the heuristics above, at levels .
  • Those pages are checked for the signs of tampering listed on the right.
  • Each sign adds points (hover a line on the right). The total gives the page a risk rating: Suspicious from , High risk from , Critical from points or more.
  • Critical and High risk open one window that lists every sign.
  • Suspicious warns at level only outside safer regions, and everywhere at levels .
  • “Always ignore on this site” in a warning adds “Tamper: site” to your allow list.

Allow list

One per line: Category: domain. All = no protection and no warnings at all (the site is locked at level ); Download, Firewall, Scam and Tamper = that tab's warnings are ignored and its blocks allowed on the site. A domain on its own counts as All. On Save the list is sorted, and All for a domain removes its other lines. “Always allow” / “Always ignore” in a warning adds a line.
